## Review-status meanings

```text
draft
The record is being assembled or challenged.

literature audited
The main source claims have been checked and candidate changes proposed.
This is not human expert approval.

expert reviewed
A named scientific reviewer has accepted or revised the record.

approved
The record has passed source, claim, product, governance and release review.

retired
The claim or mapping should no longer be used.
```

## Prepublication policy

Quality arXiv, PsyArXiv and bioRxiv sources may influence model design and research priorities. They must remain visibly labelled as preprints and cannot alone validate a product, clinical route or consequential use.
