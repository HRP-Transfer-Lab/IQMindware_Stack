# 1. Required correction

The IQ Mindware Reasoning layer must not be described as if it were only a SMART-style or Relational Frame Theory-style programme.

The intended architecture contains at least three related but distinct lineages:

```text
A. FORMAL / RELATIONAL REASONING
recover and combine abstract relations
→ validity
→ analogy
→ constraint
→ probability
→ wrapper transfer

B. EXPLICIT APPRAISAL AND BELIEF RESTRUCTURING
identify the active appraisal or belief
→ distinguish observation from inference
→ examine evidence
→ generate alternatives
→ recalibrate probability and confidence
→ test the model in behaviour
→ update after the outcome

C. COGNITIVE REAPPRAISAL
reinterpret the emotional meaning of a situation
→ alter the affective response
```

These lineages overlap in working memory, alternative generation, evidence evaluation and metacognition, but they are not interchangeable.

The governing correction is:

> **Cognitive restructuring is an applied explicit-reasoning intervention family, not merely another formal reasoning wrapper and not a synonym for general reasoning capacity.**

This distinction is already compatible with the IQ Coach specification, which permits domain-semantic Reasoning to support evidence-sensitive reframing while keeping CBT-adjacent use outside therapy, diagnosis and treatment claims.

# 2. Reasoning intervention-family taxonomy

## 2.1 Formal and relational reasoning

**Core object:** an abstract relation, transformation, rule or argument structure.

Typical operations include:

```text
same / opposite
more / less
before / after
transitivity
analogy
identity substitution
conjunction
constraint
valid / invalid / cannot tell
probabilistic path inference
```

Relevant evidence families include formal reasoning, belief bias, argument mapping, natural-frequency reasoning, Relational Frame Theory and SMART.

Typical outcomes are:

```text
formal validity sensitivity
relational discrimination
wrapper recovery
lure resistance
argument quality
reasoning benchmark performance
```

Formal reasoning training does not by itself constitute CBT or cognitive restructuring.

## 2.2 Professional belief calibration

**Core object:** a claim, explanation, forecast or decision model.

Typical operations include:

```text
separate validity from believability
separate source quality from familiarity
weight independent evidence
search for disconfirmation
preserve cannot-tell outcomes
calibrate confidence
accept / reject / defer / test
```

This is a non-clinical professional, educational and research route. It is not an ideology score, truth detector or clinical belief assessment.

## 2.3 Non-clinical evidence-sensitive reframing

**Core object:** an emotionally or motivationally meaningful appraisal in work, study, performance or ordinary life.

The operation is:

```text
What happened?
What interpretation became active?
What outcome is being predicted?
What evidence supports that prediction?
What evidence weakens it?
What alternative model remains plausible?
How likely is each model?
What is the smallest safe discriminating test?
What action follows now?
What happened compared with the prediction?
```

Eligible public labels include:

```text
evidence-sensitive reframing
appraisal reasoning
belief testing
prediction checking
```

A self-service product should not call this therapy, automated CBT, diagnosis or treatment.

## 2.4 Cognitive restructuring in CBT-type interventions

**Core object:** a distress-linked automatic thought, appraisal, assumption or belief.

A typical clinical sequence is:

```text
identify the emotionally active cognition
→ assess belief strength and consequences
→ examine evidence and inferential errors
→ generate a more accurate or adaptive alternative
→ test predictions through behaviour where appropriate
→ update belief, emotion and action
```

Clinical definitions commonly include Socratic questioning, thought recording, guided discovery, imagery and behavioural experiments.

The clinical literature supports cognitive restructuring as a genuine intervention family. It does **not** establish that the generic IQ Mindware Reasoning game treats depression, anxiety, insomnia or another disorder. The exact digital protocol, population, safety system, professional role, dosage and outcomes require separate validation.

Clinical use therefore belongs under:

```text
health_partner_research_v1
```

## 2.5 Cognitive reappraisal

Cognitive reappraisal is related to but narrower than cognitive restructuring.

```text
Cognitive reappraisal:
reinterpret a situation to alter its emotional meaning.

Cognitive restructuring:
identify, evaluate and modify an appraisal or belief,
often using evidence, Socratic questioning, thought records
and behavioural experiments.
```

Reappraisal may be one operation inside cognitive restructuring, but the latter normally includes a wider epistemic and behavioural process.

# 3. Evidence synthesis

## 3.1 SMART and derived relational responding

SMART provides an adjacent formal-relational training signal, not the complete foundation of the Reasoning layer.

A 2022 systematic review and meta-analysis reported:

```text
5 included studies
N = 195
non-verbal IQ effect g = 0.57
all primary-outcome studies at high risk of bias
insufficient evidence for other domains
```

The appropriate judgement is:

```text
relational-training construct rationale:
MODERATE–STRONG

SMART programme efficacy:
LOW–MODERATE / EMERGING

broad intelligence or academic transfer:
NOT ROBUSTLY ESTABLISHED

exact IQ Mindware reasoning protocol:
NOT ESTABLISHED
```

SMART should be one evidence family for formal relational reasoning, not a proxy for the whole explicit Reasoning intervention portfolio.

## 3.2 Cognitive restructuring for adult depression

A network meta-analysis of 45 studies and 3,382 participants found no evidence of differential effectiveness among cognitive restructuring, behavioural activation and combined CBT. All three outperformed care as usual and waiting list.

This supports cognitive restructuring as an active clinical intervention family while not showing that it is uniquely necessary, superior or equivalent across every condition and delivery format.

## 3.3 Cognitive restructuring for generalised anxiety disorder

A 2024 network meta-analysis of 65 RCTs and 5,048 participants found the strongest durable evidence for full CBT. The review defined cognitive restructuring as identifying and disputing maladaptive thoughts through methods including Socratic questioning, thought recording and guided imagery. Pure cognitive restructuring was not decisively active when isolated.

The appropriate inference is:

```text
full CBT for GAD:
well supported

cognitive restructuring as an isolated GAD component:
uncertain

exact IQ Mindware route:
not established
```

## 3.4 Cognitive restructuring in CBT for insomnia

A 2024 component network meta-analysis of 241 trials and 31,452 participants found cognitive restructuring associated with higher remission and improved subjective sleep quality. Possible component interactions and heterogeneous packages still require caution.

This supports condition-specific component evidence, not a universal self-service cognitive-restructuring claim.

## 3.5 Socratic questioning and cognitive change

In cognitive therapy for depression:

- therapist Socratic questioning predicted next-session symptom change in a 55-person study;
- in a 123-client study, cognitive change mediated the relationship between Socratic questioning and subsequent depressive-symptom change.

These studies support guided discovery as a plausible process of cognitive change. They are not randomised component trials proving that questioning alone is sufficient.

## 3.6 Thought records and behavioural experiments

A randomised analogue study of 91 participants found both thought records and behavioural experiments produced beneficial changes relative to control across beliefs, anxiety, behaviour and symptom-related measures.

Design implication:

> Explicit restructuring should ordinarily lead to a discriminating test or behavioural probe where safe and appropriate, rather than stopping at verbal substitution.

## 3.7 Cognitive restructuring versus defusion

An analogue study of 142 participants found brief cognitive restructuring and cognitive defusion both reduced distress relative to a negative-thought-focused control, with change associated with theoretically distinct strategies.

The ontology should therefore keep separate:

```text
change the content or appraisal
```

and:

```text
change the relationship to the thought
```

CBT restructuring, ACT-style defusion, acceptance and reappraisal must not be collapsed into one generic reframing record.

## 3.8 Reappraisal and executive function

A 2024 meta-analysis of 59 independent samples and 4,703 participants found only small associations between reappraisal and inhibition, updating and shifting (`r = .13–.19`).

This supports executive-function involvement but prevents equating a general executive-function or reasoning task with reappraisal skill.

# 4. Mapping explicit cognitive restructuring onto APC

| APC function | Cognitive-restructuring operation | Candidate measures |
|---|---|---|
| **Signal** | Identify the active thought, appraisal, image or prediction; separate event from interpretation | appraisal identification, observation–inference discrimination, active cognition capture |
| **Evidence** | Examine support, contradiction, source quality, independence and alternative explanations | diagnostic-evidence weighting, disconfirmation use, alternative-model quality, repeated-vs-independent evidence |
| **Predictive Calibration** | Estimate belief strength, probability, confidence and expected consequence; update after evidence | calibration slope, belief revision, probability revision, confidence resolution, prediction-error sensitivity |
| **Commit** | Choose accept, reject, defer, test or act; select a proportionate behavioural experiment | samples to closure, test choice, premature closure, excess checking, action threshold |
| **Compare / Update** | Compare predicted and observed outcomes and revise the appraisal model | prediction–outcome discrepancy, local versus global revision, delayed reuse |
| **Transfer / Bank** | Recover the method under a changed trigger or context and after delay | wrapper recovery, spontaneous use, implementation-intention activation, delayed re-entry |

This is not merely SMART-style relational fluency. It is explicit predictive-model revision tied to affect, action and feedback.

# 5. Product architecture implications

The Reasoning layer should expose separate protocol families:

```text
REASONING CORE

1. Formal Relational Reasoning
   symbolic, nonsense-semantic and domain-semantic structure

2. Argument and Evidence Reasoning
   evidence quality, disconfirmation and alternative models

3. Belief Calibration
   validity, believability, probability and confidence

4. Conclusion Timing
   accept, reject, defer or test

APPLIED REASONING ROUTES

5. Evidence-Sensitive Reframing
   non-clinical work, study and performance appraisal practice

6. Cognitive Restructuring Protocols
   condition-specific partner or clinical-research routes

7. Cognitive Reappraisal Protocols
   emotion-regulation routes, separately specified
```

The five-capacity Reasoning score must not be treated as a measure of cognitive-restructuring skill. Cognitive restructuring requires its own task and outcome model.

# 6. Candidate explicit-restructuring task scaffold

```text
SITUATION
What happened?

APPRAISAL
What claim or prediction became active?

EFFECT
What emotion, urge or behaviour followed?

EVIDENCE
What supports the appraisal?
What conflicts with it?
Is the evidence independent or repeated?

ALTERNATIVES
What other models fit the facts?

CALIBRATION
How likely is each model?
How confident should you be?

TEST
What is the smallest safe test that distinguishes the models?

ACTION
What proportionate action follows now?

UPDATE
What happened compared with the prediction?

TRANSFER
Can the same method be recovered under a different cue and after delay?
```

Required lures should include:

```text
possibility treated as probability
emotion treated as evidence
one event treated as a general rule
repeated evidence treated as independent evidence
plausible alternative treated as proven
reassuring alternative accepted without evidence
catastrophic endpoint with omitted intermediate paths
familiar source treated as universally reliable
behavioural test that cannot discriminate between models
```

Do not train:

```text
negative sentence
→ positive sentence
```

as if positivity were the target. The target is a more accurate, useful and testable appraisal. A balanced alternative may remain negative, uncertain or conditional when that is what the evidence supports.

# 7. Measurement model

Separate at least four outcomes.

## 7.1 Appraisal identification

```text
Can the active interpretation be distinguished from the observable event?
```

## 7.2 Evidence-sensitive revision

```text
Does belief change in proportion to diagnostic evidence?
```

## 7.3 Affective and behavioural consequence

```text
Does the revised appraisal alter distress, avoidance, checking,
approach or another prespecified functional outcome?
```

## 7.4 Transfer and deployment

```text
Can the process be used with a new trigger,
under emotional load,
in a real or simulated behavioural probe,
and after delay?
```

Candidate trial-level fields:

```text
initial_belief_probability
initial_confidence
emotion_intensity
action_urge
evidence_source
evidence_independence
evidence_diagnosticity
alternative_model_count
alternative_model_quality
revised_belief_probability
revised_confidence
selected_test
test_discriminability
selected_action
predicted_outcome
observed_outcome
post_outcome_update
wrapper
delay
support_level
safety_flag
```

A simple balanced-thought-quality score is insufficient.

# 8. Governance and safety

## 8.1 Non-clinical route

Eligible when:

- the target is ordinary work, study or performance appraisal;
- examples are low risk;
- the system does not diagnose a distortion or disorder;
- no symptom-treatment claim is made;
- the user can decline, stop or choose another route;
- escalation language is available when high-risk content appears.

Public label:

```text
Evidence-Sensitive Reframing
```

## 8.2 Health or clinical route

Required when:

- the target is depression, anxiety, insomnia, trauma, pain or another health condition;
- the system aims to reduce symptoms or modify clinically significant beliefs;
- content includes high-risk material;
- cognitive restructuring forms part of a care pathway.

Required controls include:

```text
condition-specific protocol
clinical or service leadership
ethics and data governance
safety and escalation plan
eligibility and exclusion rules
professional oversight
adverse-event process
versioned intervention content
validated outcome measures
human decision authority
```

The generic Reasoning Coach must not be marketed as automated CBT.

# 9. Digital and LLM-assisted delivery

Digital delivery is plausible, but evidence for a dialogue dataset or AI conversation system is not equivalent to clinical efficacy.

Preprints such as Crisp, template-guided Socratic-inquiry systems and synthetic CBT dialogue frameworks may inform:

```text
dialogue-state design
question-sequence design
resistance modelling
quality-assurance rubrics
clinician-review tools
```

They cannot alone establish:

```text
therapeutic efficacy
safety
equivalence to a therapist
eligibility for self-service clinical use
```

The safest initial IQ Mindware implementation is deterministic or tightly templated. LLM support should be reserved for contexts with explicit governance, monitoring and human review.

```text

professional_belief_calibration
nonclinical_evidence_sensitive_reframing
clinical_cognitive_restructuring
cognitive_reappraisal
behavioural_experiment_belief_update
```

Preserve separate fields for:

```text
construct_basis
intervention_category_evidence
exact_protocol_validity
condition_specificity
workflow_or_functional_transfer
governance_profile
```

Do not infer clinical evidence from the Reasoning core:

```text
strong Reasoning construct basis
+
moderate CBT cognitive-restructuring evidence
≠
validated IQ Mindware clinical intervention
```

The exact bridge requires a versioned applied protocol and direct study.

---

# 12. Promotion recommendation

1. Adopt the separate intervention-family taxonomy.
2. Retain all new records at `draft`.
3. Add clinical evidence as category-level evidence only.
4. Obtain review from a CBT-qualified clinician or researcher before any health-route promotion.
5. Prototype the non-clinical evidence-sensitive reframing scaffold using neutral work/study scenarios.
6. Test whether users distinguish event, appraisal, evidence, alternative, probability, test and action.
7. Add behavioural experiments only when safety and discriminability are explicit.
8. Maintain a separate clinical protocol family for condition-specific research.
9. Keep SMART evidence confined to the formal-relational branch.
10. Update public Reasoning copy so it spans formal structure, belief calibration and governed appraisal revision.
