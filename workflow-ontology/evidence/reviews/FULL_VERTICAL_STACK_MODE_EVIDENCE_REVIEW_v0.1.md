# Full Vertical Stack and Control-Mode Evidence Review

**Version:** draft v0.1.0  
**Date:** 8 August 2026  
**Status:** research review and candidate-registry specification; not approved for production claims  
**Scope:** Attention Control → Relational Memory → Binding Memory → Path Prediction → explicit Reasoning, crossed with Signal, Evidence, Predictive Calibration and Commit  
**Includes:** formal reasoning, domain-semantic verbal reasoning, belief calibration and evidence-sensitive reframing  
**Parent architecture:** Adaptive Predictive Control / SECC and the IQ Coach five-capacity stack  
**Machine-readable companion:** `../vertical-stack-mode-matrix.candidate.json`  
**Candidate-source companion:** `../vertical-stack-sources.candidate.json`

---

## 1. Purpose

This review extends the evidence programme from G Track and the currently available Signal-mode apps to the full planned vertical stack:

```text
Attention Control
→ Relational Memory
→ Binding Memory
→ Path Prediction
→ Reasoning
```

Each capacity is crossed with four recurring control functions:

```text
SIGNAL
recover the relevant representation under interference

EVIDENCE
infer and update the representation from sequential evidence

PREDICTIVE CALIBRATION
judge how strongly the current prediction should be trusted

COMMIT
act at a context-sensitive threshold
```

Predictive Calibration is cross-cutting and should be derived mainly from Evidence and Commit behaviour. It is not a fourth public game.

The review asks five different questions for every cell:

```text
1. Is the target construct supported?
2. Has the exact IQ Mindware task been validated?
3. Does training change a separate cognitive outcome?
4. Does any change reach a meaningful workflow or applied outcome?
5. Is the proposed public use eligible under the relevant governance profile?
```

These questions must not be collapsed into one evidence score.

---

## 2. Overall conclusion

The five-layer architecture is defensible as a **research and product-development map**, but the evidence is uneven across the 20 capacity-by-mode cells.

The strongest current positions are:

```text
Attention Signal
strong task/construct anchor + moderate emerging training evidence

Relational Memory Signal
strong construct basis + exact app and transfer still under validation

Binding Memory Signal
strong paradigm basis + conflicting broad-transfer evidence

Path Prediction
strong computational and learning-science basis + exact app unvalidated

Explicit Reasoning
strong formal and educational basis + exact graph-to-language protocol unvalidated
```

The Evidence, Calibration and Commit columns have substantial scientific foundations in sequential sampling, adaptive learning, metacognition, confidence and stopping-control research. However, the exact IQ Mindware implementations are still principled extensions and should remain **in development** until task validation and controlled training studies are completed.

The public position should therefore be:

> **The full stack is evidence-structured, not uniformly evidence-proven. Each module carries its own construct, implementation, training and transfer status.**

---

## 3. Evidence grading used here

| Level | Meaning in this review |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | Multiple direct studies, a high-quality synthesis or a mature computational/experimental literature supports substantially the same construct. |
| **Moderate** | At least one direct study or several close studies support the interpretation, but important design or transfer questions remain. |
| **Plausible but not established** | A defensible theory-led or adjacent-evidence rationale exists, but the exact mapping or task has not been directly tested. |
| **Not established** | No adequate evidence yet supports the exact product benefit or transfer claim. |
| **Not eligible** | The claim would imply diagnosis, treatment, guaranteed transfer or another use outside the intended governance boundary. |

A public claim cannot be stronger than the weakest essential link in its chain.

# 4. Layer 1 — Attention Control

## 4.1 Signal

The masked Majority Function Task provides the direct scientific anchor for extracting a majority relation under interference and limited exposure. The current evidence supports strong mechanism fit and moderate emerging training evidence, including selected attention-task and verbal-learning transfer in one randomised study.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: STRONG
exact Attention Coach implementation: MODERATE / UNDER VALIDATION
separate cognitive training evidence: MODERATE / EMERGING
workflow transfer: NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: AVAILABLE NOW
```

Public wording:

> Attention Coach Signal trains controlled extraction of a majority relation under visual interference and time pressure.

Boundary:

> Evidence is strongest for the trained mechanism and selected cognitive-task transfer, not sustained everyday focus or workplace performance.

## 4.2 Evidence

Sequential-sampling and adaptive-learning research supports a distinct Evidence Control function. People can update learning rates following change points; control over when to stop sampling can improve evidence processing and choice accuracy; and confidence can interact with accumulation bounds.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: STRONG
exact IQ Mindware task: PLAUSIBLE, NOT ESTABLISHED
training evidence: NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: IN DEVELOPMENT
```

The task should test source-specific histories, volatility changes, diagnostic versus redundant samples and recovery after hidden context shifts.

## 4.3 Predictive Calibration

Metacognitive sensitivity and calibration are measurable but should not be inferred from accuracy or mean confidence alone. Adaptive metacognitive training has shown domain-general improvements in metacognitive ability, providing a positive training signal for this column. Confidence-regulated accumulation and dynamic learning provide further mechanistic support.

Required design:

```text
confidence conditional on evidence strength
prediction-error sensitivity
learning-rate adaptation
out-of-sample probability calibration
meta-d′ or comparable model-based efficiency
```

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: STRONG
exact derived profile: PLAUSIBLE, NOT ESTABLISHED
general calibration-training evidence: MODERATE
product status: PLANNED DERIVED PROFILE
```

## 4.4 Commit

Evidence-accumulation research supports stopping as a separable control problem. Free control over stopping can improve processing, while desired conclusions can bias both accumulation and stopping. The current Signal task does not directly train this function.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: STRONG
exact task: PLAUSIBLE, NOT ESTABLISHED
training evidence: NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: IN DEVELOPMENT
```

The task must distinguish:

```text
premature commitment
proportionate commitment
excess sampling
forced choice at maximum samples
deadline omission
technical interruption
```

# 5. Layer 2 — Relational Memory

## 5.1 Signal

Relational integration is strongly associated with higher-order reasoning and can predict fluid reasoning beyond several conventional working-memory tasks. Analogical reasoning draws jointly on relational integration, active maintenance and interference resolution.

The current Relational Memory Signal task therefore has strong construct fit. However, association is not training transfer, and broader working-memory-training evidence remains small, task-similarity-sensitive and mixed.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: STRONG
exact WM Coach implementation: MODERATE / PROVISIONAL
separate training evidence: PLAUSIBLE / MIXED
workflow transfer: NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: AVAILABLE NOW
```

Core error signatures should include wrong-lag lures, relation reversals, same-feature/wrong-relation lures and cross-wrapper relation recovery.

## 5.2 Evidence

Comparison can promote relational-category learning and transfer to surface-dissimilar items. This supports a future mode in which several examples imply a latent relation and the learner must distinguish noise, exceptions and genuine changes.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: MODERATE
exact task and training benefit: NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: IN DEVELOPMENT
```

The first validation should compare:

```text
single-example training
versus
comparison and contrast training
```

using held-out relational categories and explicit change-point trials.

## 5.3 Predictive Calibration

Trial-level confidence during n-back is sensitive to load and lure interference, yet participants may remain overconfident and show weak discrimination between correct and incorrect responses. This makes relational calibration a meaningful target rather than an automatic consequence of accuracy.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: MODERATE
exact profile: PLAUSIBLE, NOT ESTABLISHED
general metacognitive-training evidence: MODERATE
product status: PLANNED DERIVED PROFILE
```

## 5.4 Commit

The relevant problem is deciding when a relation is stable enough to use or report. Repeated checking research is highly relevant to the slow-compensatory pole: repeated checking can reduce memory confidence substantially while producing much smaller accuracy changes.

This evidence should inform the task design, not support a treatment claim.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: MODERATE
exact task and training effect: NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: IN DEVELOPMENT
```

# 6. Layer 3 — Binding Memory

## 6.1 Signal

Visual working-memory research provides a strong paradigm basis for maintaining feature conjunctions and detecting changes. Source-monitoring research provides a broader framework for remembering the origin, context and conditions attached to information.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: STRONG
exact G Track / WM Coach implementation: MODERATE / PROVISIONAL
broad training transfer: CONFLICTING / NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: AVAILABLE NOW
```

The task should preserve separate error classes:

```text
same relation, wrong colour
same colour, wrong relation
correct information, wrong source
correct rule, wrong context
partial-match acceptance
swap errors
wrong-lag conjunction
```

## 6.2 Evidence

A future Evidence mode should reveal features, sources and contexts progressively and require the learner to construct or revise a binding. The construct is supported by source-monitoring and associative-learning literatures, but the exact intervention remains a principled extension.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: MODERATE
exact task and training evidence: NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: IN DEVELOPMENT
```

## 6.3 Predictive Calibration

Binding confidence should reflect conjunction completeness, source reliability and context stability. It must not be reduced to a self-report confidence average. Calibration should be analysed separately for complete matches, partial matches and swaps.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: MODERATE
exact profile: PLAUSIBLE, NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: PLANNED DERIVED PROFILE
```

## 6.4 Commit

The relevant stopping problem is deciding when the source–claim or feature–context binding is sufficiently verified. The intervention must experimentally vary error cost, checking cost, reversibility and source reliability rather than infer commitment from reaction time alone.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: MODERATE
exact task and training evidence: NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: IN DEVELOPMENT
```

# 7. Layer 4 — Path Prediction

## 7.1 Signal

The Path Prediction layer has a strong computational foundation in transition learning and successor representations. The signal task is not simply anomaly detection: it must distinguish expected, rare-but-valid, context-invalid and impossible transitions.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: STRONG
exact IQ Mindware implementation: PLAUSIBLE, NOT ESTABLISHED
training evidence: NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: IN DEVELOPMENT
```

## 7.2 Evidence

Probabilistic successor-representation models explicitly address uncertainty, context inference and switching among task-specific maps. Human work also suggests that people flexibly balance temporally abstract SR-like prediction and more detailed model-based simulation according to environmental predictability.

A future task can therefore test:

```text
transition learning rate
context inference
change-point recovery
rare versus impossible discrimination
one-step versus multi-step prediction
```

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: STRONG
exact task and training evidence: NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: IN DEVELOPMENT
```

## 7.3 Predictive Calibration

Path calibration concerns confidence in transition probabilities, context identity and reachability. It should be represented as calibrated probability and uncertainty, not as a neural-precision claim.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: STRONG
exact profile: PLAUSIBLE, NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: PLANNED DERIVED PROFILE
```

## 7.4 Commit

The path-level decision is whether to sample, explore, follow, switch or stop. The general problem is well supported by value-of-information, active-sampling and model-arbitration research, but the exact training protocol remains untested.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: STRONG
exact task and training benefit: NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: IN DEVELOPMENT
```

# 8. Layer 5 — Explicit Reasoning

## 8.1 Signal: formal and verbal reasoning

The reasoning layer should separate three wrappers:

```text
symbolic
nonsense-semantic
domain-semantic verbal
```

Belief-bias research shows that believability can alter response bias and, more selectively, reasoning discriminability. The correct design is therefore not a simple accuracy score. It should model:

```text
validity sensitivity
acceptance bias
belief congruence
cannot-tell discrimination
lure false acceptance
wrapper recovery
```

Relational integration, analogical comparison and argument-mapping research provide a strong general basis for explicit relational reasoning and structured argument evaluation. The exact IQ Mindware item bank and graph-to-language transfer protocol remain unvalidated.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: STRONG
exact task: PLAUSIBLE, NOT ESTABLISHED
adjacent instructional/training evidence: MODERATE
workflow transfer: NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: IN DEVELOPMENT
```

A current bioRxiv preprint reports a dissociation between formal logical reasoning and the natural-language network. This is useful design evidence for keeping formal validity and verbal-semantic wrappers separate, but it must remain a prepublication watchlist source.

## 8.2 Evidence: argument and belief revision

The planned Evidence mode should present premises, counterevidence and alternative explanations sequentially. It should test whether the learner:

```text
weights diagnostic evidence
separates repeated from independent evidence
searches for disconfirmation
preserves cannot-tell outcomes
revises confidence proportionately
```

Argument mapping has a substantial educational literature and a recent systematic review, but evidence is heterogeneous and does not establish transfer from a short cognitive game to consequential judgement. Natural-frequency formats improve Bayesian reasoning relative to conventional probability formats, but performance remains far from universal.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: STRONG
exact task: PLAUSIBLE, NOT ESTABLISHED
adjacent training evidence: MODERATE
workflow transfer: NOT ESTABLISHED
```

## 8.3 Predictive Calibration: belief calibration

Belief Calibration should be a distinct derived profile:

> Is confidence in a conclusion proportionate to the validity, independence, coverage and diagnosticity of its evidence?

It is not:

```text
a truth detector
an ideology score
a clinical belief assessment
a scalar confidence score
```

The profile should separate discriminability from response bias and use trial-level confidence. Adaptive metacognitive training provides the strongest positive general training signal. Motivated-reasoning research shows why the task must also test desirable versus undesirable conclusions: people may require less evidence for conclusions they want to believe.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: STRONG
exact profile: PLAUSIBLE, NOT ESTABLISHED
general calibration-training evidence: MODERATE
product status: PLANNED DERIVED PROFILE
```

## 8.4 Commit: conclusion timing

The Commit task should allow the learner to:

```text
accept
reject
defer
request another diagnostic sample
propose a test
```

The target is a minimum sufficient model, not maximum speed or certainty. Desired conclusions, sampling costs and reversibility should be experimentally manipulated.

Current judgement:

```text
construct basis: MODERATE–STRONG
exact task and training evidence: NOT ESTABLISHED
product status: IN DEVELOPMENT
```

# 9. Reframing and reappraisal

Explicit verbal reasoning can support a **non-clinical evidence-sensitive reframing** application, but this must remain distinct from psychotherapy.

The defensible non-clinical operation is:

```text
initial interpretation
→ alternative interpretation
→ evidence for and against each interpretation
→ confidence calibration
→ small behavioural or informational test
→ revision after feedback
```

Relevant evidence includes:

- small task-performance benefits from stress-arousal reappraisal and stress-is-enhancing mindset interventions;
- evidence that people with anxiety or depression can often perform reappraisal, although group differences and mechanisms vary;
- digital reappraisal studies that mainly inform delivery burden and engagement rather than proving a generic app effect;
- cognitive-bias-modification evidence showing that interpretation bias can change more reliably than mental-health outcomes.

This supports three different route classes:

| Route | Website status | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Evidence-sensitive reframing for work/study** | Evidence-informed, pilot-ready | Non-clinical performance or education; no symptom-treatment claim |
| **Professional belief calibration** | Evidence-informed, pilot-ready | Test validity, believability, evidence and confidence separately |
| **Interpretation-bias or clinical reappraisal protocol** | Partner/research only | Requires condition-specific evidence, ethics, safety and professional governance |

The website must never convert category-level CBT, CBM-I or reappraisal evidence into direct evidence for the exact IQ Mindware reasoning app.

# 10. Vertical-transfer evidence

The strongest evidence supports component relations, not a proven five-stage causal ladder.

Examples include:

```text
relational integration ↔ fluid reasoning
working-memory maintenance/interference ↔ analogical reasoning
comparison → relational-category transfer
predictive maps → context-sensitive future-state learning
formal structure + semantic wrappers → belief-bias and transfer tests
```

The full vertical-transfer claim remains a Trident-G research hypothesis:

```text
extract the signal
→ hold the relation
→ preserve the binding
→ learn the path
→ reason explicitly with the same structure
```

To validate it, the programme must use the same versioned relation family across layers and test whether earlier-layer performance predicts later-layer acquisition, wrapper recovery and delayed performance. Correlation alone is insufficient; the decisive evidence will come from controlled cross-layer interventions and held-out transfer.

# 11. Prepublication watchlist policy

High-quality arXiv, PsyArXiv and bioRxiv sources may inform:

```text
model design
measurement alternatives
candidate neural/computational mechanisms
pre-registered replication targets
open analysis pipelines
```

They may not, by themselves:

```text
upgrade a public claim to strong
validate an IQ Mindware implementation
justify clinical or consequential routing
override stronger peer-reviewed contrary evidence
```

Current watchlist examples include:

- Kean et al., *Evidence from Formal Logical Reasoning Reveals that the Language of Thought is not Natural Language*, bioRxiv, DOI `10.1101/2025.07.26.666979`;
- Baram et al., *An abstract relational map emerges in the human medial prefrontal cortex with consolidation*, bioRxiv, DOI `10.1101/2024.10.11.617652`;
- Hyland and Albarracin, *On the Variational Costs of Changing Our Minds*, arXiv `2509.17957`.

All material public use must retain the preprint status where it materially supports a claim.

# 12. Recommended research sequence

## Study A — task and measurement validation

For each new cell:

```text
construct validity
trial-yield and data quality
reliability curves
model comparison
confidence calibration
lure-specific error signatures
device and timing transport
```

## Study B — mode dissociation

Within each capacity, test whether Signal, Evidence and Commit produce separable behavioural parameters and whether Predictive Calibration adds information beyond accuracy and speed.

## Study C — vertical prediction

Use held-out participants to test whether earlier-layer parameters predict learning or transfer at the next layer.

## Study D — controlled training

Compare:

```text
Signal-only
Evidence-only
Commit-only
combined mode
active control
```

Use separate cognitive and functional outcomes.

## Study E — wrapper and delayed transfer

Test symbolic, nonsense-semantic and domain-semantic reasoning, held-out graph compositions and delayed recovery.

## Study F — governed reframing pilot

For non-clinical work or education, compare evidence-sensitive reframing with an information-only control. Health-related versions require a separate partner/research protocol.

# 13. Website translation

The website should show each capacity and mode separately.

Example:

```text
REASONING — EVIDENCE
Construct basis: strong
Exact IQ Mindware protocol: in development
Adjacent training evidence: moderate
Workflow effect: to be tested
Status: evidence-informed research route
```

It should not say:

```text
The full stack is scientifically proven.
Reasoning Coach changes beliefs.
The app treats anxiety through reframing.
The vertical pathway raises IQ.
```

The constructive commercial position is:

> **Each route has a defined cognitive target, an evidence level, an implementation status, a separate outcome plan and a route for becoming better validated.**
