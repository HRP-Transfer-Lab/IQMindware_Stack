# Study 001 — Workflow Ontology Content Validation

**Status:** planned  
**Study family:** ontology development and content validation  
**Programme plan:** [`../../docs/RESEARCH_VALIDATION_PLAN.md`](../../docs/RESEARCH_VALIDATION_PLAN.md)

## Working title

**Development and Content Validation of a Cognitive Workflow Demand Ontology for AI-Assisted Knowledge Work and Higher Education**

## Initial workflow stages

1. Verify an AI-generated report before release.
2. Recover a complex task after interruption.
3. Integrate sources into an evidence-based assignment.
4. Screen studies against eligibility criteria.

## Aims

1. Establish the relevance and completeness of the workflow stages, demand dimensions and friction signatures.
2. Assess agreement on mappings from demands and frictions to APC capacities and functions.
3. Assess agreement on person-, task/interface-, team/process- and organisation/system-level intervention loci.
4. Test whether the ontology produces sensible, availability-aware and claims-safe routes for held-out cases.

## Planned sequence

```text
bounded evidence review
→ cognitive task analysis
→ draft coding manual
→ independent double-coding
→ multidisciplinary expert panel
→ content-validity and agreement analysis
→ held-out route cases
→ ontology v0.2 candidate
```

## Evidence dimensions targeted

Primary:

```text
W — workflow representation
D — cognitive-demand mapping
F — cognitive-function mapping
I — governance definition and initial acceptability
```

Not established by this study alone:

```text
P3/P4 — separate cognitive or functional training transfer
O2–O4 — intervention or deployment effectiveness
```

## Progress gate

The study is ready to move to behavioural validation when:

- the workflow models are recognisable to performers;
- critical stages and failure modes are sufficiently comprehensive;
- expert mappings and dissent are documented;
- alternative workflow and system explanations have been considered;
- unsupported mappings remain hypotheses;
- held-out route cases pass claims and governance review.
